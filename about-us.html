About Us

Located just 15 miles North of downtown Austin, Advanced Machining has been servicing Central Texas businesses with high quality, tight tolerance CNC machined parts for 20+ years.We pride ourselves in ensuring your satisfaction – whether that’s through part quality, fabrication quickness, thorough communication, or thinking on our feet, our focus will always be on delivering product exactly how you need it.

Over the years, we’ve worked on parts for a wide range of applications, with much of our experience coming from the semiconductor industry. We’ve made components for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, including parts for Applied Materials, as well as hardware used to hold probe components in probe card assemblies. Our work has also gone into medical devices, aerospace applications, and even equipment that has been used in space!

Along with machining, we have extensive experience in CAD/CAM programming and provide engineering support when a part requires more than simply machining to a print. Whether you already have a finished design or need help working through how a part can be made, we can work with your team to find a practical approach

Our shop features 8 total vertical CNC mills, ready to handle your high volume production needs.
